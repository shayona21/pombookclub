What we're about

A little tart, a little sweet — like the fruit.

We started Pomegranate because we missed the specific joy of finishing a book and immediately needing to talk about it with someone. Each month we pick one book — classic, new, uncomfortable, funny — and get together to pull it apart over snacks and (occasionally) real arguments. We're spread across NYC and Singapore, so we make video calls work.

Co-founded by Diya Gandhi & Shayona Basu.
